Public intermediate CA certificates issued by Samsung Electronics for Tizen TV
development (as shipped with Samsung's Tizen Studio TV extension and redistributed by
Apps2Samsung). They contain no private keys; they complete the certificate chain in
package signatures.

- vd_tizen_dev_author_ca.cer – Samsung VD Author CA
- vd_tizen_dev_public2.crt   – VD DEVELOPER Public CA Class
- vd_tizen_dev_partner2.crt  – VD DEVELOPER Partner CA Class
